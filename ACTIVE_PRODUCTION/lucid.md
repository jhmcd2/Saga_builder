## 🚗 2. Competitive Conquest & Trade-In Bonuses
Lucid targets luxury combustion and EV owners aggressively. Note that you cannot stack Loyalty and Conquest together. [5, 8] 

* 
* Competitive Conquest Credit ($2,000): Available if you currently own or lease any non-Lucid vehicle brand (Tesla, Porsche, BMW, Mercedes, Rivian, etc.). You just show a copy of your current registration. No trade-in required. [5, 6, 11]
