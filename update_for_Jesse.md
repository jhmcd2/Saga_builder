The most effective, medical-grade path to winning that argument without paying for monthly subscriptions involves specific steps:

The Clinical Blueprint: How to Prove White Coat Syndrome

The Instinct Crossover is a distinct engineering deviation that embeds physical, luminescent analog clock hands over a digital display cutout. Because Garmin significantly overhauled its internal hardware layout for this model, its storage architecture changed:
• Microphone & Speaker: Pass. Just like the standard version, it is entirely mic-free and speaker-free.
• Wi-Fi / LTE / Camera: Pass. No internet-facing hardware or cameras are present.
• The Accessible Memory Rule: PASS (With a Setting Adjustment). Unlike older Garmins, the Instinct Crossover allows you to manually lock down its USB architecture. Avoid the AMOLED version. Garmin upgraded the memory on the AMOLED Crossover to a massive 4GB and forced its USB mode into MTP (Media Transfer Protocol). This means the AMOLED model will always force-mount itself as a media player device on a computer, breaking your accessible memory requirement.
